# PIXEL SPRINT: ONE-PAGER

**Game Name:** Pixel Sprint
**Type:** Endless Runner
**Main Action:** Swipe

## 1. Title
Pixel Sprint is an ultra-simple mobile game where you swipe left or right on your screen to move out of the way of blocks and run as far as you can.

## 2. Aesthetics
The game offers simple fun with easy-to learn controls that test your quick reflexes. It builds excitement by running slightly faster the longer you stay alive.

## 3. Core mechanics
You drag your thumb left or right on the screen to switch your character between two lanes. Your main goal is to dodge red blocks sitting directly in your path.

## 4. Dynamics
Your character automatically runs forward down the track at all times. The game reuses a few basic road pieces continuously to keep everything running smoothly without stuttering. If you hit a block, you lose instantly and can tap the screen once to play again.

## 5. Progression & content
Each run lasts for a short 30 to 60-second try, making it quick to play. The entire game uses one basic street environment made from 5 simple road pieces. Your single best score is automatically saved directly on your phone storage.

## 6. Platform features
The game is built for easy one-thumb controls while holding your phone vertically in portrait mode. Your phone buzzes once with a quick vibration when you crash. All game text and scores are placed carefully to stay clear of camera notches at the top of your screen.

## 7. Performance budget
The action stays locked at 60 frames per second so your phone never gets hot while playing. The game uses very little RAM so it never lags or crashes. It opens instantly in under one second from your home screen.

## Short description (76 / 80 characters)

Swipe to switch lanes and dodge blocks in a quick one-thumb runner. Offline.

## Long description

Run, swipe, survive. Pixel Sprint is an ultra-simple endless runner built for quick 30 to 60 second runs on your phone.

HOW IT PLAYS
• You run forward automatically. Swipe left or right to switch lane.
• Dodge the red blocks in your path. Hit one and the run is over; tap once to go again.
• The run gets slightly faster the longer you survive.
• Your best score is saved on your phone, so there is always a number to beat.

MADE FOR PHONES
• Portrait, one hand, thumb only.
• Swipes are measured in dp, so they feel the same on any screen size.
• Text and score stay inside your phone's safe area, clear of notches, punch-hole cameras and the gesture bar.
• A short vibration when you crash. You can turn haptics off.
• Pause at any time with the on-screen button or the Android back gesture. The game also pauses itself when you leave the app or pull down notifications, and only resumes when you tap Resume.
• Locked at 60 fps.

ACCESSIBILITY
• Small, Normal and Large text sizes.
• Haptics on or off.
• Large touch targets for every menu button.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
• Plays fully offline.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
• No personal data is collected. Your settings and best score stay on your phone.
